# Geography For Kids

**Geography For Kids** is an educational geography website built to make exploring our world fun, accessible, and easy to understand.

**Website:** https://geographyforkids.site
**YouTube:** https://youtube.com/@geographyeducationkids

## About

Geography For Kids is a growing educational resource covering geography from around the world.

Explore topics including:

* Countries
* Continents
* Cities
* Capitals
* Flags
* Mountains
* Oceans
* Rivers
* Deserts
* Volcanoes
* Rainforests
* Islands
* Climate
* Animals and ecosystems
* Maps and borders
* Earth science
* Geographic history
* Interesting geography facts

The goal is to create a large, trustworthy geography resource for students, families, teachers, and anyone curious about the planet.

## Features

* Fast article search

## Reliable Information

Accuracy is a major priority.

Information should be researched using reliable sources such as government organizations, scientific institutions, universities, museums, and established educational publications.

Examples include:

* Encyclopaedia Britannica
* NASA
* NOAA
* U.S. Geological Survey
* United Nations
* World Bank
* National Geographic
* Government geographic agencies
* Universities and museums

Geography For Kids does **not** intend to copy articles from these sources. Information is presented in original educational writing with appropriate attribution.

## Goals

The long-term goals of Geography For Kids are to build:

* A massive geography article library
* Interactive educational maps
* Country and continent databases
* Geography quizzes
* Educational games
* Classroom resources
* A large YouTube channel
* Automated but human-reviewed content production
* A reliable geography reference for kids and families
